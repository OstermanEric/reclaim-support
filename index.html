Start with your settings

Open Reclaim’s Settings to review Protected apps, Unlock length, Emergency passes, and Sources & rates. Sources & rates shows the current earning rules, daily caps, connection status, and each source’s Check now action.

Keep your local history: do not delete and reinstall Reclaim as a troubleshooting step. The current app does not offer cloud recovery for your time balance or streak.

Sources & rewards

Apple Health rewards are missing

First check that the activity appears in Apple Health. In Apple Health, tap your profile, find Apps → Reclaim, and review read access for steps, flights climbed, and workouts. Return to Reclaim → Settings → Sources & rates → Apple Health and use Check now. Health updates can arrive later from a watch or another source, and partial permission affects what Reclaim can read.

Strava is connected, but an activity did not earn time

Open Sources & rates → Strava and choose Check now. Reclaim verifies eligible activity recorded after connection; it does not backfill earlier workouts. Manual, flagged, deleted, too-short, duplicate, or capped activity may not add time. Walks use Apple Health steps; an activity shared through Health and Strava should not earn twice. If the connection has expired, disconnect and reconnect. Send the activity date/type and the message shown if you still need help—never a token or route.

Todoist tasks are not appearing

Open Sources & rates → Todoist, check whether All tasks or One project is selected, and use Check now. Reclaim counts eligible tasks you completed today, including tasks created today. Connection or scope changes start a new eligibility baseline, so earlier completions are not backfilled. Tasks completed by someone else in a shared project do not qualify. Check the current daily cap and selected project; contact support before repeatedly changing scope.

Lichess puzzle rewards are missing

Check the connected username in Sources & rates → Lichess. Solve puzzles with that Lichess account, then return to Reclaim or use Check now. Only successful solves after connection count, and the current batch requirement and cap are shown in Sources & rates. If authorization expired, disconnect and reconnect; earlier activity is not backfilled.

My activity was verified, but my balance did not increase

Check the current wallet capacity and source cap in the app. A duplicate activity, a full balance, an unmet reward threshold, or a daily source cap can prevent a new credit. Read the reward receipt or notice for the reason. Focus blocks need to finish before awarding time, and free passes do not count as earned minutes for a streak.

A source cannot connect or says it is unavailable

Check your internet connection and try again later. Provider outages or authorization limits can affect Strava, Todoist, and Lichess. Your existing local wallet and Screen Time protection do not require a live provider connection. If the problem persists, send the source name and exact error; never send authorization codes or access tokens.

App protection & passes

Protected apps are not blocking

Open Settings → Protected apps and confirm your selections. Review Screen Time access in Reclaim and iOS Settings. If access was revoked, authorize it again. Earned time or an active emergency pass can temporarily allow access; wait for that allowance to end and check again. If protection still fails, report which kind of target was selected and whether an allowance was active.

What does Unlock length change?

Unlock length controls the length of an earned-time session. Check its description in Settings for the current behavior. Your earned balance and session allowance are separate from emergency passes.

How do emergency passes work?

The current app provides limited five-minute emergency passes. Check how many remain in Settings → Emergency passes and confirm in Reclaim before starting one. The blocked-screen button opens Reclaim; it should not spend a pass on its own. Passes do not add earned time or count toward your streak. If a pass runs beyond its displayed end time, include the start/end time and whether the phone was locked or Reclaim was backgrounded in your report.

Reward notifications are missing

Review Settings → Reward notifications in Reclaim and notification permission for Reclaim in iOS Settings. Focus modes and sound settings can also affect delivery. Turning off notifications does not remove earned credits.

Your account & local history

I cannot sign in

Choose Continue with Apple and use the same Apple Account you used with Reclaim. Hide My Email may mean your Reclaim account uses an Apple relay address. The current app does not offer email/password sign-in or a password-reset screen. If you used an earlier email/password or phone account, contact support for help with that older account or deletion; do not assume Apple sign-in will restore its history.

Can I restore my balance after reinstalling or switching phones?

Reclaim’s current wallet, streak, and detailed history are local to the iPhone. Signing in again does not restore them from Firebase. Removing the app or changing phones can erase that history; an iOS device backup may behave differently, but Reclaim does not provide or guarantee cloud wallet recovery. For troubleshooting, keep the installed app and contact support.

What happens when I sign out or disconnect a source?

Signing out does not clear local history or automatically stop Screen Time enforcement. Disconnecting a source stops future syncs and removes its Reclaim connection credentials when the operation completes; previously earned minutes and local reward records remain. Disconnecting Reclaim does not delete the provider’s account.

How do I delete my Reclaim account?

Open Settings → Delete account and confirm with Apple. The flow disconnects connected sources, removes known older cloud records and your Firebase account, revokes Apple authorization, and clears local history and protected-app selections. If deletion reports an error or you cannot sign in, contact support so we can help complete it. Removing the app alone does not delete your account. Your separate Apple, Strava, Todoist, and Lichess accounts are unaffected.

For data handling and privacy requests, read the Reclaim privacy policy.

Send a useful support request

These details help us find the issue without asking for private activity:

  • Your Reclaim version and build number, plus your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
  • The source or feature involved, any error message, and approximate time of the issue.
  • A screenshot if useful, with account, task, health, and app-usage details you do not want to share removed.

Never send passwords, Apple authorization codes, OAuth access/refresh tokens, raw Health records, routes, or Screen Time tokens. We will not ask for them.

Email ejosterman@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as practical. If you use an Apple relay address or cannot access an older account, tell us; we can arrange an appropriate way to verify a deletion request.

TestFlight testers can also submit feedback with a screenshot through TestFlight. Include the build and a short description of what you were doing.
