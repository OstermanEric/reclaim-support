How can I help?

Get help with your account, Screen Time controls, rewards, Health access, Lichess, or anything else in Reclaim.

Contact support

Send an email and include the details below. You'll receive a reply as soon as practical.

ejosterman@gmail.com

What to include

These details make it easier to diagnose an issue:

  • your Reclaim app version and build number;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • the steps that reproduce the issue; and
  • a screenshot, if useful, with private information removed.

Never send a password, Sign in with Apple authorization code, Health record, Screen Time token, or Lichess access token.

Quick help

I can't sign in

Confirm that you're using the same sign-in method you used when creating the account. If you signed up with Apple, choose Continue with Apple. For an email-and-password account, use the password-reset option and check your spam folder.

Walking or workout rewards are missing

Open the iPhone Settings app, then go to Privacy & Security → Health → Reclaim and confirm the requested read access is enabled. Open Reclaim again after changing access. Health rewards are based on eligible data that Apple Health has already recorded.

Screen Time controls aren't working

Open Reclaim and review its Screen Time setup and selected distractions. If iOS asks for Screen Time authorization, approve it. Also confirm that Screen Time is enabled on the iPhone. Restarting Reclaim after changing authorization can refresh the current state.

Lichess activity isn't appearing

Disconnect and reconnect Lichess from Reclaim's settings, then complete the authorization flow again. Reclaim only requests the Lichess permission it needs for puzzle activity.

My balance changed after reinstalling

Sign in with the same Reclaim account and allow recovery to finish. Reclaim syncs a compact recovery state rather than uploading raw Screen Time, HealthKit, or detailed Lichess activity.

Privacy and account deletion

Read the Reclaim Privacy Policy for details about what stays on your device and what is stored with Firebase.

To permanently delete your Reclaim account, open Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app. If you cannot access the app, email ejosterman@gmail.com from the address connected to your account.

TestFlight feedback

If you're testing Reclaim through TestFlight, you can also send beta feedback and attach a screenshot from the TestFlight app. Include a short description of what you were doing when the issue occurred.
